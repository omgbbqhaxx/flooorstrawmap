flooor strawmap

A strawman roadmap for the flooor protocol on Base. Cobalt lands in September 2026. Everything after it is a sketch, not a promise.

What is the strawmap?
A single page that puts every planned flooor upgrade — the auction contract, the royalty vault, and the surfaces people actually touch — on one timeline. It exists so a change in one layer can be read against everything it depends on, instead of living in a separate thread.
Who is this for?
Holders, bidders, collection creators, and anyone building on top of the flooor contract. It is dense on purpose. If you just want to bid, sign, and claim, go to flooor.fun; the documentation covers the mechanics.
What is shipping next?

Cobalt, September 2026. Three headliners:

  • parallel epochs — each listed collection runs its own auction clock rather than sharing one
  • weighted split — your share of the vault scales with the value you signed, not with headcount
  • agent wallets — a scoped spend permission signs and claims every epoch without a wallet popup
What do the letters on top represent?
Releases are named on an incrementing first letter, mineral-style. Cobalt is the next one with a finalized name. The releases after it — D*, E* and F* — are placeholders (D* is pronounced "D star") and will be named as their scope firms up.
What do the rows and colors represent?

Upgrades are grouped into three layers: auction (A), vault (V), and surface (S). Dark boxes are headliners, dashed boxes are offchain work, faint boxes have already shipped, and the black boxes on the right are north stars — directions with no date attached.

Time runs left to right. Anything to the right of Cobalt is scope, not schedule.

What are headliners?
The one upgrade per layer that a release is actually about. Capping it at one keeps the cadence honest: a release ships when its three headliners are done, and smaller items ride along if they are ready.
What are the north stars?
  • everyday floor: every collection gets a real, honest price every single day
  • zero-click yield: sign and claim with no popup, no staking, no lockup
  • open listing: any collection can open a flooor without asking permission
  • royalties to holders: 100% of the vault goes to the people who show up
  • agent-native: the whole protocol reachable by an agent, not just a browser
What is the time frame?
Roughly two releases a year through 2028. Cobalt has a date because it is built; D*, E* and F* carry quarters as an ordering hint. Treat them with healthy skepticism — a single dependency slipping moves everything to its right.
Why "strawmap"?

A portmanteau of "strawman" and "roadmap". The strawman qualifier is deliberate:

  1. It is a discussion starter. Publishing a shape people can argue with beats publishing nothing.
  2. It is a work in progress. Scope moves between releases, and items get dropped when they turn out not to matter.

The strawmap is not a prediction. It is one coherent path among many.

Will it change?
Yes. Expect a revision at least every quarter, with the date noted in the legend at the bottom of the map.
Can I share feedback?
Please do — especially if you hold, bid, or build on flooor. Open an issue on GitHub or reply to @flooordotfun.
